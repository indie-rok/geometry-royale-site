Privacy Policy

Geometry Royale is developed by Emmanuel Orozco ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

The short version

We collect nothing. There are no developer servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind in Geometry Royale.

Player name and colour

You choose a player name and colour in the app's settings. This is stored only on your device.

Nearby play

Nearby matches use your device's local network (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) via Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework, with no internet connection and no server. Hosting or joining a nearby match shares your chosen player name with the other players in that match, directly between devices. Nothing is sent to us.

Online play

Online matches use Apple Game Center. Game Center is Apple's service, governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not this one. Playing an online match shares your Game Center player identifier and nickname with the other players in that match through Apple's servers; the identifier is used to agree on which device hosts the match and is never stored.

Leaderboards and match log

The local Endless Solo and Endless Group leaderboards and a diagnostic match log are stored only on your device. The group leaderboard keeps the chosen names of the teammates in each run. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

Children

Geometry Royale has no user accounts and no chat or messaging feature. The only information ever shared with other players is a self-chosen name in nearby play, or your Game Center nickname and player identifier in online play; the typed name is never sent online.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
