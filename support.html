Support

Everything you need to play Geometry Royale, and what to do if something isn't working.

Controls

  • Left thumb moves your fighter
  • Right thumb aims and fires
  • DASH button — two charges, get in, get out, or get away
  • Tap a weapon pad to pick it up; tap again to throw it

Modes

  • Practice — a solo test range with every weapon on its own pad and a dummy
  • Endless Solo — survive waves alone, chase a score, climb the local top-10 leaderboard
  • Endless Group — the same waves with 2 to 4 players; teammates can revive each other, and a shared shop pad sells run-wide gun upgrades (damage, fire rate, magazine, pierce, plating)
  • Versus — 2 to 4 players, free-for-all, first to 5 kills

Hosting or joining a match

Endless Group and Versus need 2 to 4 devices. One player hosts and gets a 4-digit room code; everyone else enters that code to join.

  • Nearby — host and joiners must be on the same Wi-Fi network, or have Bluetooth turned on. No internet connection is needed.
  • Online — host and joiners sign in to Game Center and enter the same room code to be matched over the internet.

Game Center

Game Center sign-in is optional. It's only needed for Online matches — Practice, Endless Solo, and Nearby play all work without it.

Troubleshooting

  • Can't find a nearby match: check that Local Network access is allowed for Geometry Royale in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network, that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (or have Bluetooth on), and that neither device is in Airplane Mode.
  • Can't find an online match: make sure you're signed in to Game Center under Settings > Game Center, and that both players entered the same 4-digit room code.
  • Still stuck: force-quit and reopen the app, then try again.
