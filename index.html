Geometry Royale is a top-down arena shooter for iPhone and iPad, printed in a paper-and-ink style — every player and enemy drawn as three misregistered cyan, magenta and yellow plates that drift apart on a kill. Play Practice or Endless Solo alone, or bring 2 to 4 players into Endless Group or Versus, nearby over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth or online through Game Center, joined by a 4-digit room code. No ads, no purchases, no account.
